Privacy Policy

Bird Roots

Last updated: 22 October 2026

In short: Bird Roots collects no personal data. Nothing you play or hear is recorded, stored on a server, or shared with anyone. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

Microphone

The Listen feature uses your iPhone's microphone only while you have tapped Start listening. The sound is analysed live, on your device, to describe what kind of call a bird is making. Audio is never saved to a file and never leaves your device.

Field journal and settings

Your field journal (the list of interpreted calls), your pads, patterns and sound settings are stored only on your device. Deleting the app deletes them.

Fonts

When you are online, the groovebox loads two typefaces (Fraunces and Outfit) from Google Fonts. Like any web request, this tells Google's servers your IP address; no other information is sent. Offline, the app uses built-in fonts instead.

Children

Bird Roots does not collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.

Contact
