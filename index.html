Bird Roots

22 voices from the edge of extinction

Bird Roots is a groovebox built from the voices of 22 of the world's rarest and most ancient birds, plus Listen, a field guide that hears the birds around you and explains what kind of message each call carries.

Need help? Open a support request (a free GitHub account is needed), and we usually answer within a few days.

Common questions

I don't hear anything. Tap Enter the grove first. Sound plays even with the silent switch on, so check the volume buttons.

How do I put a bird on a pad? Select a pad, then tap + next to a species in the list. On iPad you can also drag a species onto a pad.

Does Listen really translate birds? No app can: birds don't use words. Listen tells you the kind of call (song, alarm, hawk alarm or contact call) from its sound, and explains what that kind of call is for, with a confidence level.

Listen says it heard music or speech, not a bird. On purpose: it only interprets sounds your iPhone recognises as birds. Get closer, point the phone toward the bird, and avoid wind on the microphone.

Is my audio recorded? Never. Everything is analysed live on your device. See the privacy policy.

Are these real recordings? No. The groovebox voices are synthesized impressions shaped from descriptions of each bird's call, so they can be played and transformed like an instrument.
